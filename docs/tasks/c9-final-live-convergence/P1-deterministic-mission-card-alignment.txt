MISSION CARD

- active task/work unit;
- work-unit completion condition.

MUST PRESERVE
- applicable requirements/invariants;
- effective permission/stop constraints.

OBSERVED

- current SHA/text-framing/path facts already observed;

- George-observed current-stage facts only.

- still-valid replay-safe observations.

- pending George-owned validation that follows implementation.
